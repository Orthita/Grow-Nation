About Grow Nation

Train. Transform. Triumph.

An industrial consulting & training organization based in Mohammadpur, Dhaka, Bangladesh.

Our story

Closing the gap between a standard and a habit

Grow Nation was founded in February 2026 by Anup Sarkar, a food engineer and Master Trainer with more than eight years of running plants and training teams in Bangladesh's FMCG sector.

We train workers, supervisors and managers, prepare factories for audits and export certification, run career programmes for students and support rural communities with skills that lead to income. Every programme ends with a measurable result and a certificate anyone can verify online.

Practical learning, honest assessment, standards-based content and certificates that hold up to scrutiny.

Train. Transform. Triumph.

Practical, standards-based training for workers, supervisors and managers, from hygiene basics and HACCP to Lean tools and leadership skills. Delivered in the classroom, on the floor and online, in Bangla and English.
